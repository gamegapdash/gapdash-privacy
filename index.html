We receive information needed to validate purchases and deliver virtual items (such as product identifiers and transaction status). We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

  • Analytics data — We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the App is used (for example screens viewed, gameplay events, and session information). Analytics is configured with non-personalized ads settings where supported.

On Android, the App may include third-party libraries that declare access to the Google Advertising ID. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.
